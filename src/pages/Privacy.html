Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

Welcome to ristoikonen.com. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website and login via the Google Authentication Platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in to our application using your Google Account, we use the Google OAuth 2.0 authentication service. Through this platform, we only request access to specific, limited information necessary to create and manage your account. This includes:

  • Primary Email Address: Used to identify your account and communicate important system updates.
  • Full Name / Display Name: Used to personalize your user profile experience within the app.
Important Note on Google Data: We do not collect, store, or have access to your Google Account password. Google handles the authentication securely on their own servers and simply passes us a unique token to verify your identity.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data retrieved from your Google Account strictly for the following purposes:

  • To provision, maintain, and secure your user account.
  • To personalize your user dashboard and application interface.
  • To send essential transactional or administrative emails regarding your account.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is only shared under the following conditions:

  • With Your Consent: We may disclose information if you explicitly authorize us to do so.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose data if required by law, subpoena, or government regulation.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policyincluding the Limited Use requirements.

We retain your information for as long as your account remains active. You can disconnect your Google account from our application or request total data deletion at any time by contacting us at the email provided below. Upon request, we will delete all associated data from our active databases within [e.g., 30 days].

We implement commercial-grade security measures to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction of your data. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.
